V0 produce stats_only sistematicamente basso (mean 5.60 vs atteso ~6). Causa: neutri di pass/duel/dribble settati teoricamente (80/50/50) non corrispondono alla realtà del dataset. Iteriamo con neutri empirici.
Task 26 — Calcolare i neutri empirici dei 3 indicatori percentuali
sqlSELECT
  ROUND(AVG((stats_json->'passes'->>'accuracy')::numeric), 2) AS pass_accuracy_mean,
  ROUND(STDDEV((stats_json->'passes'->>'accuracy')::numeric), 2) AS pass_accuracy_std,
  ROUND(AVG(
    CASE WHEN (stats_json->'duels'->>'total')::int >= 5
         THEN (stats_json->'duels'->>'won')::numeric / NULLIF((stats_json->'duels'->>'total')::numeric, 0) * 100
    END
  ), 2) AS duel_win_rate_mean,
  ROUND(STDDEV(
    CASE WHEN (stats_json->'duels'->>'total')::int >= 5
         THEN (stats_json->'duels'->>'won')::numeric / NULLIF((stats_json->'duels'->>'total')::numeric, 0) * 100
    END
  ), 2) AS duel_win_rate_std,
  ROUND(AVG(
    CASE WHEN (stats_json->'dribbles'->>'attempts')::int >= 2
         THEN (stats_json->'dribbles'->>'success')::numeric / NULLIF((stats_json->'dribbles'->>'attempts')::numeric, 0) * 100
    END
  ), 2) AS dribble_success_mean,
  ROUND(STDDEV(
    CASE WHEN (stats_json->'dribbles'->>'attempts')::int >= 2
         THEN (stats_json->'dribbles'->>'success')::numeric / NULLIF((stats_json->'dribbles'->>'attempts')::numeric, 0) * 100
    END
  ), 2) AS dribble_success_std
FROM player_giornata_stats
WHERE (stats_json->'games'->>'minutes')::int >= 30;
Filtro a 30' per evitare rumore da minutaggi minimi che distorcono i tassi.
